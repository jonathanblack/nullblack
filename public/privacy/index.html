privacy

Effective November 18, 2026. Jonathan Black, doing business as nullblack.

This covers nullblack.com and the games below. Contact hello@nullblack.com.

This website

This site does not use analytics, ads, or accounts. If you email us, we keep your address and your message so we can reply. We do not sell that mail.

Faded Maps

Your puzzles, streaks, and settings stay on your device. Older daily puzzles are dropped after about two weeks.

The website and the iOS and Android apps can show a Google ad under the results. On iOS, the app may ask permission to track you across other apps. You can say no and still see ads. Those ads are not tied to an account. There is no account and no leaderboard.

Sheerfall

On sheerfall.com, Google may show an ad after a run. You can choose to watch an ad to reroll a reward. The daily challenge does not offer that.

We count how often things happen in the game, such as a visit or an ad view. Those counts are not tied to your name. A leaderboard uses a device id and the name you choose, plus your score. Online play sends your name and what you do in the match, and a token so you can rejoin if you drop.

Cyberdeck: Runtime

Your progress stays on your device. You can copy a save out as text and paste it back later. There are no ads and no account.

Cyberdeck: Packet

You can play with a save on your device. You can also make an account with an email or a short code. We email a sign-in code, and we keep that email or code, the device, and your cloud save. Signing out of one device does not delete the account. The iOS and Android apps use the same account if you sign in.

Jurassic Legacy

Your expedition stays on your device. A daily run does not overwrite it. You can make an account with an email or a short code. We email a sign-in code, and we keep that email or code, the device, and your cloud save. When you ask for a code, we also count attempts from your network address so we can slow down repeated tries. That count is not part of your account. Signing out does not delete the account.

The daily leaderboard uses a random id stored on your device and the name you choose, plus your score. Spending in the lab uses the game's own currency, not real money. The game does not show ads.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email hello@nullblack.com and we will delete it. Faded Maps and Sheerfall show ads.

Your rights

Where GDPR, CCPA, or a similar law applies, you can ask what we hold about you, for a copy, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop sale or sharing for advertising. We do not sell personal information. Google may use device data to show ads in Faded Maps and Sheerfall, under Google's own policy. Email hello@nullblack.com with the game and, if you have one, the account email or code. We will not treat you worse for asking.

Deletion

Clear the game's saved data, or uninstall the app, to remove what is only on your device.

To delete a Jurassic Legacy or Cyberdeck: Packet account, email hello@nullblack.com from the address on the account, or include the code. Signing out is not deletion. This email is how you ask for deletion on Google Play and elsewhere until a button exists in the game.

To remove a Sheerfall leaderboard name, email us with the name and the game. There is no button for that in the game.

Who else sees data

  • Google, for ads in Faded Maps and Sheerfall.
  • Apple and Google, for the iOS and Android apps, including Apple's tracking prompt on iOS.
  • Our host, for the website, saved accounts, sign-in attempt counts by network address, leaderboards, email, and Sheerfall's online matches.

How long we keep it

What is on your device stays until you delete it. Sign-in codes expire after 15 minutes. Account details, cloud saves, sign-in attempt counts, and leaderboard entries stay until you ask us to delete them or we no longer need them to run the game.

Changes

When this policy changes, we will update the date above. If we have your email and the change matters, we will write to you.
